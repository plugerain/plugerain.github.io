Week of 2026-11-08

2026-11-08

What I focused on this week

  • Spent a lot of time reorganizing and polishing career-related documents (resumes, cover letters, and similar materials) across several tracks.
  • Rewrote past work experience (technical support projects, store-operations incentive design) into more concrete, narrative descriptions.
  • Prepared the paperwork needed to participate in an education mentoring activity.

What I learned about how I work

  • Leaving a key document unupdated for a while means facts drift out of sync, and I only notice the mismatch later. Important documents need to be refreshed as soon as the underlying status changes.
  • Describing experience in terms of role and process, rather than dressing it up with numbers, turned out to be more accurate and holds up better over time.
  • In weeks with several events clustered together, checking the exact timing in advance saves a lot of last-minute scrambling.

Direction for next week

  • Stop deferring pending submissions and decisions — close them out this week.
  • Prepare concretely for upcoming interviews and assessments.
  • Keep key reference documents updated in parallel with everything else.
