Week of 2026-10-25

2026-10-25

What I focused on this week

  • Instead of writing documents from scratch each time, I set up a few base templates and built variations from them.
  • I gathered scattered recurring edits into one list and planned to handle them all in a single focused session.
  • I tried to set clearer criteria for what to prioritize when several things are moving at once.

What I learned

  • When records disagree with each other on outcomes or timing, it's better to go back to the source than to just let it slide.
  • For repetitive tasks, building a base template in advance saves more effort than redoing the work each time.

Direction for next week

  • Keep refining the base document system and finish the batch of pending edits.
  • Set clearer priorities across the several things currently in progress at once.
