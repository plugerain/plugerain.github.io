Project

Digital Freight Brokerage

Instant pricing and first-accept matching between shippers and truck owners

Next.jsPostgreSQL (Supabase-compatible)PGliteTypeScriptFlutter (planned)Funding simulator
Role
Business planning, requirements and funding plan; built with AI agents (vibe coding)
Period
2026-10 ~

Problem

  • Freight brokerage still runs on phone calls and group chats. Dispatch is slow, rates are inconsistent and truck owners are paid late.
  • Small brokers rely on manual work, so they find it hard to scale.

Solution

  • Instant pricing: a shipper's order is priced on the spot. B2B shippers use contract rates.
  • Sequential offers: the order goes to up to five qualified truck owners in turn. The first to accept gets the contract immediately.
  • Completion and payout:
    • Drop-off photos and a signature create an electronic proof of delivery, and the shipper leaves a satisfaction rating.
    • The truck owner receives the fare minus the fee (8% less a satisfaction incentive). Payouts run at 18:00 the same day or 09:00 the next day.
  • When nobody accepts: a company-run truck takes the job, so no order goes unserved.

What I did

  • Defined the business rules, fee and incentive structure, and payout cycle from a brokerage practice guide.
  • Built a funding simulation from one-off costs, monthly costs and revenue assumptions to size the required capital.
  • Set up a requirements system that marks undecided items as "provisional" or "open" instead of guessing.
  • Built the database, business rules and automated checks with an AI agent.

Technology & architecture

  • Business rules (offer order, accept race, fee calculation, payout batches) are PostgreSQL functions. They pass 65 automated checks.
  • The structure is Supabase-compatible and runs on PGlite on a PC, so testing needs no installation.
  • The admin system and shipper web app use Next.js, and the driver app will use Flutter.

Next

  • Admin system, shipper web app and document guide (stage 4, in progress)
  • Truck-owner app (Flutter)
  • Integrated testing on phones
